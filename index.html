The thesis

Product · Payments
Partnerships · Delivery

A banking product only counts when money actually moves through it. My work sits in the space between a business idea and a working transaction — writing what needs to exist, choosing who builds it, and staying with it until customers use it.

I started on the branch floor at Mercantile Bank and moved into mobile financial services, then digital banking, then cards and digital payments. That path means I read a product from both ends: the regulatory and operational reality a bank has to live with, and the integration detail a fintech partner needs to ship.

Day to day that looks like BRDs, PPGs, SRSs, RFPs and SoPs; commercial negotiation with vendors; UI/UX specifications; business cases and cost-benefit analysis; business UAT; and post-live monitoring with the MIS to prove it worked. Computer science degree, MBA in management information systems, JAIBB and AIBB — the technical vocabulary and the banking one, on purpose.
